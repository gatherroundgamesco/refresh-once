Privacy policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Summary

Refresh Once does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or browsing data. It has no analytics or tracking. The extension cannot read page content.

Data stored on your device

Timer settings, per-site preferences and unlock status are stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage) and never leave your device.

Payments

Payments for the optional one-time unlock are processed by ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com) and Stripe. When you choose to pay, ExtensionPay and Stripe handle your email and payment details under their own privacy policies. Refresh Once never sees your card details.

License checks

The extension makes no network requests unless you click "Unlock". At that point it creates a random, anonymous license key with extensionpay.com and opens the payment page. After that, when you open the popup, it asks extensionpay.com (using only that key) whether the unlock has been paid. No browsing history, page content or personal data is sent. The key and an install date are stored in your browser's extension storage.
